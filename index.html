Areas of expertise

Chartered Accountant with 8 years of experience in audit, costing, statutory advisory, internal control and income tax compliance.

Audit

Planning and carrying out audits of accounts and records. This means checking transactions against supporting documents, confirming that financial statements give a true and fair view, and reporting findings clearly to management.

Costing

Working out the true cost of products and services by preparing cost sheets and cost records. This helps a business set the right prices, control expenses and see where it is making or losing money.

Statutory Advisory

Guiding businesses on the legal and regulatory requirements that apply to them, such as company law and tax law, so that filings are correct, deadlines are met and penalties are avoided.

Internal Control

Reviewing how a business handles cash, purchases, sales and approvals, and recommending checks that reduce errors, misuse and fraud while keeping day-to-day work running smoothly.

Income Tax Compliance

Computing tax, filing returns, making tax payments on time and supporting clients through assessments and notices, with attention to tax planning within the law.

Areas of expertise

Audit Executive with 8 years of experience in accounting, GST filing, bookkeeping, costing, internal audit and SOP documentation.

Accounting

Maintaining ledgers and preparing accounts so that every transaction is recorded in the right head, and the books are ready for financial statements, audits and tax filing.

GST Filing

Preparing and filing GST returns on time, matching purchase and sales invoices with returns, and checking input tax credit so that clients pay the correct amount of tax.

Bookkeeping

Recording day-to-day entries, organising bills and vouchers, and reconciling bank statements, so that a business always has up-to-date and reliable books.

Costing

Maintaining cost records and working out the cost of production or service, to help management understand expenses, control wastage and price correctly.

Internal Audit

Carrying out regular checks of transactions and processes during the year, finding gaps or weak points early, and reporting them with practical suggestions to fix them.

SOP Documentation

Writing clear, step-by-step procedures for routine tasks such as billing, payments and record keeping, so that work is done consistently and new staff can learn quickly.
